# E. coli benchmark source review

Reviewed 17 October 2026. This is a bounded source review for a replicated analysis and method comparison; it does not claim that ChronoClade has reproduced either paper.

## Australian One Health study: primary benchmark

Watt et al., “Parameters for one health genomic surveillance of *Escherichia coli* from Australia,” Nature Communications, online 2 January 2025, DOI `10.1038/s41467-024-55103-2`. The article uses 5,471 Australian genomes and reports 827 STs. Its analysis combines cgMLST and SKA: chewBBACA 2.8.5 against the exact public EnteroBase `Escherichia.cgMLSTv1` scheme (accessed 19 July 2022), Coreugate 2.0.5 and RapidNJ 2.3.3 for allele distances/tree, then SKA 1.0 on ten selected STs. The reported ≤40-allele and ≤100-SNP groupings are study-specific exploratory criteria. The paper warns that their groups are not direct-transmission clusters.

The 2025 publisher correction explicitly replaces Supplementary Data 1 because the original version was incorrect. The corrected file resolves 5,471 named sample rows, with 5,471 BioSamples and BioProjects, 4,248 SRA accessions, and 1,223 assembly accessions. Its ST column yields 794 distinct values (5,443 numeric STs plus 28 records with the same “Novel – reads unavailable for assignment in Enterobase” label), not the paper’s reported 827 STs. The correction did not state that any result text changed. Keep the replacement as the metadata authority and log the unexplained count gap.

The corrected source file is `validation/beta/ecoli-onehealth/sources/41467_2025_58600_MOESM1_ESM.xlsx`, SHA256 `384b910cd031c9d2b24d437c680fe1d6768b71b621204512f2fc6ec09ab7fbb3`. The superseded workbook is retained only for audit (`41467_2024_55103_MOESM3_ESM.xlsx`, SHA256 `68d5cce74ee8036894d92bd3f6d5b52e8a7b8df3c95b86a7314591670208ea02`). Supplementary Data 2 pair distances are in `41467_2024_55103_MOESM4_ESM.xlsx`, SHA256 `fd932077bbeff71364241b44f0fe27e11fe7713c2dcc15f90c41aeea2309354b`; the correction page HTML is SHA256 `e9caa440ea7b45b40bff2039c537cc5e3182aa70a93c0e69fbe3f5ad7a493a3d`.

The metadata distinguishes source niche and source type; it does not give city-level locations. Year is the supported date precision. Do not represent food or environmental samples as host taxa. The reported temporal analyses of ST131 (root-to-tip R² 0.0003), ST1193 (0.02) and ST95 (0.09) are legitimate negative/unsupported targets; BEAST analyses for ST131 and ST95 failed to converge to stationarity.

The frozen 30-sample target is numeric ST95 network cluster 6 in Supplementary Data 2: 30 samples, 224 pairs at ≤100 SNPs, with 15 food, 11 human and 4 livestock source labels over 2010–2016. Paper prose gives 16 food in its highlighted 30-strain cluster, an internal 1-record inconsistency. The eight-assembly native pilot is selected before calls and includes group labels 260, 304, 477, 665, and 895 in the publication’s ≤40 allele cgMLST clustering field. It spans Human, Food and Environmental source niches and ACT/NSW. The exact assemblies are downloaded and their ENA BioSample references match the corrected metadata. This pilot tests native caller behavior and published distance/group expectations, not per-locus concordance against an unpublished reference profile.

## Positive temporal candidate: Irish ST131 study

Ludden et al., “Genomic surveillance of *Escherichia coli* ST131 identifies local expansion and serial replacement of subclones,” Microbial Genomics 6(4), 2020, DOI `10.1099/mgen.0.000352`, PMCID `PMC7276707`. The paper combines 69 LTCF genomes, 35 additional Irish genomes, and 690 global genomes (794 total). The official Supplementary Table 1 provides 794 unique run accessions, country, year of isolation, broad clade, and source type; collection years span 1967–2014. It reports root-to-tip R² 0.4, an 8,567-SNP core alignment after masking mobile elements, recombination and sites with >1% missingness, and a BEAST 2.4.7 date analysis. Its biological time targets include Clade C emergence circa 1985, C2 expansion circa 1994, and the C2_8 LTCF lineage dated to 2001–2005 with observed local expansion from 2006.

This is a credible positive temporal method-comparison candidate. The supplement does not label a 69-row LTCF membership field, and the historical alignment/tree are not provided as a machine-readable supplementary artifact. Before freezing an LTCF-only subset, resolve membership from publication-defined sample names/accessions and verify archive records. Before a 794-genome rerun, retrieve and verify sequence/reference identities and record that results are a method comparison rather than historical reproduction.
