# Amendment 02: locus nucleotides and descriptive threshold sweeps

18 October 2026. Declared before inspecting the new locus-nucleotide distances.
The original allele and corrected-core SNP results have already been inspected;
this is an exploratory extension, not a new blinded validation.

Retain exactly the same 31 assemblies, labels and canonical 629 loci. Resolve
observed allele sequences from the frozen native reference and preserved native
calls, recording sequence availability and provenance. Reuse the existing
scope-checked direct-DNA comparator. Its equal-length, positional comparisons
are not an inferred alignment or recombination-adjusted SNP analysis. Report
missing sequence and length exclusions, with at least 599 compared loci for
eligible pairs. Do not replace missing calls with matches.

Compare three distances on matched pairs: categorical allele mismatches,
nucleotide differences within cgMLST loci, and the separately flagged corrected
core-genome SNP distances. These use different feature sets and must retain
their own denominators and units. Keep every outcome; do not fit a winner.

For each distance, sweep every integer cutoff from zero through its observed
maximum using inclusive single linkage. Process equal-weight edges together.
Preserve singleton genomes and unavailable pair evidence. Report membership
changes, cluster sizes and internal diameters, plus the independently labelled
event/control membership. A joining cutoff is not a bound on every within-cluster
pair. Identical partitions across successive sampled cutoffs describe threshold
insensitivity, not epidemiological validity or temporal persistence.

Attach observed collection-date intervals and country/host metadata to each
distinct genetic group. Dates do not affect genetic clustering. Show date
coverage and the sampled calendar envelope; fit no allele clock, temporal cutoff,
expansion statistic or transmission model on this short-event panel. Public
background/sampling-density and held-out study validation remain outstanding.

No automatic biological cutoff, product default or public-context retrieval
policy is changed by this extension. Research scripts and reports remain local.
