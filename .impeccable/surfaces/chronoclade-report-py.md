# Lineage report surface

Mode: Read. Public-health microbiologists and bioinformaticians must understand
whether dating is justified, inspect every supporting result, and carry the
evidence into review or publication. The report must work offline and in print.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A genomic case conference laid out as an evidence wall; refuse the
generic dashboard that hides inference behind summary cards.

OWN-WORLD: White working surface, near-black ink, cobalt review marks, green
pass/proceed marks, and vermillion fail/stop marks. Square sheets, hairline
rules, annotations, and large scientific figures replace soft cards and
decorative chrome.

STORY: Follow the analysis in order, see why each stage exists, inspect the
result and decision, then download the exact evidence behind it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Analysis identity and overall decision at left; the root-to-tip
scatter occupies the majority of the view; a vertical stage rail makes the
next gate unmistakable.

FORM: Genomic case-conference evidence wall, fifth grounded direction; seed
22ad0b43. Signature interaction: the static stage rail keeps the analytical
order visible while evidence manifests unfold beside each figure.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the
